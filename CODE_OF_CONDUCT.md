# Code of Conduct

ADLC Skills is a professional community of engineers, product people, and leaders working out how to build software with agents. We expect participation to reflect that.

## Expected

- Be respectful and assume good intent; disagree with ideas, not people
- Back claims with evidence, especially claims about what a skill improves
- Give credit to sources and contributors
- Keep feedback specific and actionable

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory language of any kind
- Personal attacks or sustained disruption of discussions
- Publishing others' private information
- Promotional spam in issues, pull requests, or skill content

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation for behavior that violates this code. Report concerns privately to shmulik@braightwave.com. Reports are handled confidentially.

This code is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
